# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/vanilla JS, served locally with live-server. No framework.

## Users

Prospective clients (developers, senior-living operators, business owners, property managers) evaluating an architecture and interior design firm; secondary audiences are job candidates and community partners.

## Product Purpose

Marketing website for Momentum Design Group (MDG), an architecture, planning and interior design firm in St. Paul, Minnesota. Success: a visitor understands what MDG designs, trusts the team and the work, and reaches out.

## Positioning

People-first practice ("Good people. Great spaces."): known for inventive design plus meticulous construction drawings and hands-on project oversight, across senior housing, townhomes, commercial, workplace, hospitality & retail and multifamily.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current scope: full site (home, our team, our work, our culture, moments + 2 posts, contact, on the map), keeping the original site's concepts and copy (https://mdgarchitects.com/). Project detail pages still link to the original site.
- Imagery: MDG's real photos, downloaded as local WebP in images/ (sources in images/SOURCES.md).
- Nav: our team, our work, our culture (careers), moments, contact us; footer also links on the map, careers.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Momentum Design Group. Tagline: "Good people. Great spaces." Logo descriptor: Architecture • Planning • Interior Design.
- Voice: warm, plain-spoken, a little playful ("Dare us. We love a challenge!").
- Visual: keep the original site's design (white page, Helvetica/Arial, brown #625448, rust #b44b34, original layout); work is polish, not reinvention. The user rejected a dark tackboard redesign.

## Product Principles

1. People before portfolio: the team and relationships lead the story.
2. Let the built work speak: real photography, generously shown.
3. Practical craft: communicate rigor (drawings, oversight, budget) without jargon.
